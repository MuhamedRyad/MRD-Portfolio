Process

Audience → hook → shape → print.

01

Audience

Start with one recognizable niche or profession.

02

Visual hook

Choose one central visual idea that reads quickly.

03

Shape system

Reduce the art into controlled vector forms and limited color.

04

Print check

Judge scale, contrast, placement, and clutter on a clean shirt mockup.
